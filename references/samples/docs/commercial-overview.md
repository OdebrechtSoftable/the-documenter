# Commercial Overview

Sample Project helps account teams review customer activity, track order status, and spot follow-up needs from one workspace.

## Who Uses It

| User Group | Main Need |
|---|---|
| Account managers | See customer activity before outreach |
| Sales operations | Monitor order progress and bottlenecks |
| Team leads | Review customer coverage and account health |

## Main Functionalities

- Customer activity list with status and last update.
- Order overview grouped by customer.
- Follow-up indicators for accounts that need attention.
- Basic filters for territory, status, and date range.

## Business Impact

- Reduces time spent checking customer state across separate tools.
- Helps commercial users prioritize follow-up work.
- Makes account and order status easier to discuss in team routines.

## Workflow Fit

Commercial users open the app before customer calls, during order review, or when preparing weekly follow-up lists.

## Important Limitations

- The app shows backend data; it does not own source-of-truth account records.
- Status names and order transitions come from the API.
- Export and reporting behavior depend on the configured backend.
